# ADR-0013: Autonomy as an explicit experimental objective

Status: Accepted for planning (S013), under the human's strategic-session brief.
Runtime behaviour and external-network authority are unchanged.

## Context

S001–S006e establish substantial world, recording and supervision infrastructure.
Local model pilots and directed discovery do not establish reliable self-directed
behaviour. Capability count and uptime would be inadequate scientific progress
criteria for the original question.

## Decision

Preserve all three repositories, contracts, privilege boundaries, completed work
and validation. Develop four interdependent streams: world/capabilities,
intelligence/autonomy, reliability/safety and observation/experimental science.
Strengthen S007a as local configuration-only orchestrator interchangeability;
retain specialist consultation/leasing in S007e. Implement explicit intervention
policies in S007c, then the prospective S007d baseline before major expansion.

Benchmark completion and behavioural readiness are separate: a valid negative
report completes the evidence milestone, not an autonomy claim. Freeze transparent
metrics and an engineering expansion gate; no arbitrary composite score, favourable
threshold editing or hidden steering. Use one bounded S007h improvement at a time.
S010 can proceed after a negative baseline; S012 can examine a registered null
question once validity/safety/reliability gates pass.

S006c remains mandatory for arbitrary execution, computer control, agent tools
and unattended certification, with safe local work independent of its human gate.
S007f must produce a separate approved external-provider ADR before optional
S007g; no API spend, new downloads or external access is authorised here.
The legacy continuation remains implemented as-is. The research judgement refines
ADR-0004's factual wording: factual text is not evidence of behavioural neutrality.

## Consequences and alternatives

Proceeding directly to extensive multimodal expansion risks richer tools without
a useful inhabitant. A project restart would discard functioning infrastructure.
Neither is justified. The bounded next step remains S007a, followed by policy and
baseline work; failures are retained as research evidence. S013 records this
planning session with an unused id, not completion of future implementation.
