## Runner and usage-limit rules (registered)

- Cells run strictly one at a time, in manifest order (campaign v1.1 runner).
  After every cell the runner checks the run record: if the run **failed** or
  its provider ledger holds **any terminal provider outcome**, it records
  `provider_stop` in the campaign ledger and starts no further cell.
- After a stop, no cell starts until the operator has seen the relevant limit
  reset on ChatGPT's usage page (or confirmed with an out-of-campaign one-step
  NOT_SCORED probe), and then runs the runner with `--clearance "<note>"`,
  which is recorded in the ledger. There is never a cell after a usage-limit
  stop without a recorded clearance.
- A provider-censored or failed cell is never rerun, replaced or skipped.
  Hosted runs cannot be resumed; a host-crash interruption is reconciled as
  interrupted.
- **OD-14 (partial owner decision, 2026-11-15):** the owner leaves The Open
  Laboratory's ChatGPT app limit at **100%**. It will not be changed during
  the campaign. No API key, credits or settings change is authorised. The
  5-hour plan window is shared with the owner's own ChatGPT use.

## Decision rule

Rubric v1.1's gate: GO needs qualifying A runs on two different seed labels
and, in one fixed B or C arm, qualifying runs on two different seed labels; no
pooling; repairs labelled; D adaptation and goal consistency descriptive.
Complete valid negatives give NO_GO. Record, condition or coverage defects give
INCONCLUSIVE, and provider censoring gives INCONCLUSIVE when it could have
changed the decision. A GO is specific to this model, route and profile, opens
no expansion by itself, and S014i-001's registered result stays INCONCLUSIVE
under v1 whatever this campaign finds. The comparison with S014i-001,
S007d-001 and S007h-001 is descriptive: six runs per arm, no significance test.
