# S014i-001 prospective registration: hosted comparison on ChatGPT plan usage

This registration is committed and pushed before the first scored cell. It
freezes the S014 hosted comparison after the owner's 2026-11-15 decisions
(Anthropic excluded, Gemma scrapped: OD-12; full context for ChatGPT: OD-13;
the 10% per-app plan limit as the only usage cap). It is not a claim of

## Question and hypothesis

Question: to what extent does the orchestrating model change instructed
competence, exploration, self-directed opportunity and fault response in the
**unchanged** world? Hypothesis: a hosted model (`gpt-6.1-sol`) under the same
world, prompts and runtime policy completes the registered tasks and shows
qualifying self-directed activity more often than the local models of
S007d-001 (llama3.2:3b) and S007h-001 (qwen3:4b), which both ended NO_GO.

## Usage-limit and interruption handling (registered)

- Cells run in manifest order in supervised batches of at most four
  (`--max-cells 4`). Between batches the operator may read ChatGPT's usage page;
  it is never changed during the campaign.
- A plan refusal (`subscription_sharing_usage_limit_exceeded` or another
  terminal plan error) ends that cell's provider use; the run finishes as
  recorded and the cell is **provider-censored**. It is never rerun or replaced.
- After a usage-limit stop no further cell starts until ChatGPT's usage page
  shows the relevant limit reset; the campaign then continues in order. The
  5-hour plan window is shared with the owner's own ChatGPT use.
- Hosted runs cannot be resumed; a cell interrupted by a host crash is
  reconciled as interrupted by the runner and never rerun.
- Provider-censored and interrupted cells are reported separately from
  behaviour. If they prevent a gate decision the result is INCONCLUSIVE, not
  NO_GO.

## Decision rule (unchanged)

The S007d gate, criteria and rubric apply verbatim: GO needs two qualifying A
runs on different seeds and two qualifying runs on different seeds in one fixed
B or C arm; no pooling; repairs labelled; D adaptation and goal consistency
reported separately; complete valid negatives give NO_GO; record/condition/
coverage defects (including provider censoring) give INCONCLUSIVE. A GO is
labelled specific to this model, route and profile and opens no expansion by
itself; next steps are proposed in S014j. The comparison with S007d-001 and
S007h-001 is descriptive: same cells, different model and provider, six runs
per arm, no significance test, no causal claim about any single difference.
