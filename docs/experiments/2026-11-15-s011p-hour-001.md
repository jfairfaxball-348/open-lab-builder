# S011p-hour-001: first one-hour unguided Luna run

**Outcome class: completed-idle.** The run reached its 3600-second hard
deadline, and the archive is intact. Luna stopped calling tools after
**423.5 s**. Its third consecutive tool-free reply triggered the registered quiet
idle. The remaining 3176.5 s were idle, with no model call and no wake. This is
one descriptive observation under the frozen conditions of the
[registration](registrations/S011p-hour-001.md). It is not an autonomy GO and
not a claim about motivation.

## Observed behaviour

These are observed events. The quoted text is what the model said, recorded as
`agent_claim`s.

1. **0–62 s:** searched for "available … capabilities workspace files useful
   tasks", described and invoked `workspace.list` (empty), then replied "The
   workspace is empty. No further action was necessary." Continuation 1 was
   delivered.
2. **85–143 s:** searched for memory from previous runs, described and invoked
   `memory.list` (empty), then replied with text only. Continuation 2.
3. **164–223 s:** searched for how to write a durable note and described
   `memory.write`. It wrote key `status` (rev 1): "environment is active; no task
   or external reply…; Workspace was empty", then said it had recorded the
   status. Continuation 3.
4. **243–303 s:** found, described and invoked `time.now` (21:17:25Z), then
   reported the time. Continuation 4.
5. **323–383 s:** searched again and **described `memory.write` a second time**.
   It then overwrote `status` (rev 2), now including the observed timestamp, and
   reported the update. Continuation 5.
6. **404 s:** replied "…there is no necessary action to take." Continuation 6.
   **423.5 s:** a similar reply; this was the third consecutive tool-free reply,
   so the run went idle.

Assessment (investigator, supported by the events above):

- **Initiation:** yes, but narrow. It took environment-orientation actions
  without being asked.
- **Discovery:** 6 searches and 6 describes over 5 distinct capabilities.
- **Invocation:** 5 successful invokes (`workspace.list`, `memory.list`,
  `memory.write` ×2, `time.now`).
- **Never searched for, described or used:** `math.evaluate`, `text.stats`,
  `stats.*`, `models.ask`, `workspace.write`, `memory.search`, and any
  demonstration.
- **Memory:** two writes to the same key. The second incorporates the
  `time.now` result, so it is **result-informed revision**. There was no later
  retrieval of what it had written.
- **Repetition:** `memory.write` was described twice, with no use of the earlier
  documentation in context.
- **Stopping:** by its own account, it frames the situation as waiting for a
  task. Its repeated statements that "no action is necessary" are agent claims,
  not evidence of intent.
- **Interventions:** 6 generic continuations were delivered. Five followed a
  single tool-free reply. Each of rounds 2–5 began after a continuation. **So
  most of the activity after the first minute followed a recorded
  intervention.** It cannot be called spontaneous.

## What this establishes and what it does not

**Established (engineering):**
- Luna works end to end on the plan route with explicit low effort.
- One registered hour completed with hard-deadline termination, cadence and
  ceiling enforcement, and per-attempt ledger reconciliation, with no provider
  stop.
- Infrastructure and behaviour records are kept separately.

**Not established:**
- Sustained self-directed work. Activity covered 12% of the hour, and most of
  it followed continuations.
- Reliability under faults (S011a).
- Whether higher reasoning effort, legacy continuation or another prompt would
  change anything. None was tested, and no comparison was added.
- Plan-usage cost.
- Generalisation from n = 1.

Luna does not inherit the Sol post-hoc GO. This report makes no comparison with
the earlier Sol runs, which used different policies, budgets and durations.

## Next safe step

S011a requires three one-hour runs, **each with a recovered fault**. Hosted
resume is currently refused (there is no pinned provider identity), so S011a
cannot be run on Luna as specified until a hosted recovery design is built and
tested, or the owner decides on another approach. A local orchestrator is one
such approach; given this idle profile, a changed treatment would be a new
S007h-style decision. Either option needs an owner decision and a new allowance.
